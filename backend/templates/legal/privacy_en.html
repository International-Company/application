This policy explains what we collect and why, what we do not collect, who we share it with, and your rights. The Arabic version prevails in case of any discrepancy.

1. What we collect

DataSourceWhy
Your TikTok account identifier, display name, avatar, and follower count TikTok, through official login, with your permission To prove the account is yours and link your requests to it
Your phone number Selected by you from your device, verified by a code A second pillar of your identity, and to contact you about requests
Device identifier, model, OS version, and integrity check result Your device To bind your session to one device and block tampered devices
Content of TikTok notifications related to withdrawals Your device, with your explicit permission To know that your withdrawal started, was sent, or was rejected
Your withdrawal requests, amounts, statuses, and transfer references The platform To deliver the service and keep an accurate financial record
Your acceptance of the terms: version, time, IP address, device fingerprint At the moment you accept Legal proof of exactly what you agreed to

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not read notifications from any app other than TikTok. The notification service ignores everything else, and the package signature is verified before any notification is accepted.
  • We do not read your messages, contacts, photos, or location.
  • We never see or ask for your TikTok password.
  • We do not take screenshots or record your screen.
  • The autofill service operates only inside the TikTok app and never reads what you type elsewhere.

3. How we protect your data

  • Your TikTok access tokens are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM and are never sent to your device.
  • All communication with the server is fully encrypted.
  • The financial ledger and audit log are append-only: they cannot be modified or deleted, by us or anyone else.
  • Access to user data is limited to operations staff who need it, and is fully logged.

4. Who we share with

  • The Egyptian receiving account holder: sees your request code, its amount, and your display name in order to confirm arrival. They do not see your phone number or device data.
  • Technical service providers: hosting, SMS delivery, push notifications, and device integrity checks — each strictly within what its function requires.
  • Authorities: only upon a binding legal request.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Retention

  • Financial records: kept for the period Egyptian law requires for accounting records.
  • TikTok tokens: deleted when you unlink or when they permanently expire.
  • Captured notification content: kept while the related request is open, then reduced to what the financial record requires.
  • Audit log: kept permanently, as it is append-only by design.

6. Your rights

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw your consent and unlink your TikTok account at any time.
  • Request deletion of your data, except financial records we are legally required to keep.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the support channels in the app.

7. Permissions the app requests

8. Children

The service is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, a new version is issued with a new number, and users are notified in the app before it takes effect.
